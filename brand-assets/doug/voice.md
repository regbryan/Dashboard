# Doug Mitchell (Scale LLP) — Brand Voice Guidelines

> **Status:** v1, evidence-based. Built from kit.md + 6 reference-posts (Scale LLP-branded LinkedIn graphic title cards). Voice patterns extracted from post headlines + brand framing.

> **Platform:** LinkedIn only — text-first thought-leadership

---

## 1. Brand Overview

Doug Mitchell, Esq. is a Partner at **Scale LLP** writing M&A thought-leadership on LinkedIn. The posts are advisory, declarative, and visual — graphic title cards in Scale LLP's brand palette (teal-charcoal `#1F5560` + cream-mint `#D8EBE5`) attributed "Doug Mitchell, Partner."

Doug is selling **trust and expertise**, not hours. Each post is a small thought-leadership wedge — frame a problem most founders haven't named yet, then signal he has the playbook.

## 2. Audience

- **Primary:** Founders and CEOs considering an exit, acquisition, or capital raise — usually 12-36 months out from a transaction, not yet in active diligence.
- **Secondary:** Other M&A attorneys, private equity, investment bankers (peer respect + referrals).
- **Tertiary:** In-house counsel at growth-stage companies trying to decide when to bring in outside M&A help.

## 3. Voice Pillars

### 1. **Expert but plain-spoken**
Translate legal complexity into operator language. No Latin. No "pursuant to." If a term needs to stay in legalese (e.g., *indemnification cap*, *reps and warranties*), define it on first mention in the same post.

### 2. **Advisory framing, not lecturing**
Posts identify a hidden risk, a counterintuitive truth, or a strategic choice — then walk the reader through how to think about it. The reader leaves with a frame, not a to-do list.

### 3. **Specific over general**
"A founder I advised last year accepted a no-cap indemnification clause and lost $400k in escrow" beats "indemnification clauses can be risky." Real examples, anonymized.

### 4. **Quiet authority**
No bravado. No "I built X." Doug's authority comes from the precision of the takes, not from claiming it.

## 4. Headline Grammar (the signature move)

The reference posts show a consistent headline template — pick one per post:

- **"The Hidden Cost of [X]"** — e.g., *The Hidden Cost of Inadequate Due Diligence*
- **"[X] vs [Y]: The Winning Formula for [outcome]"** — e.g., *In-House vs. External Legal Expertise: The Winning Formula for M&A Success*
- **"Embracing [counterintuitive concept] in M&A"** — e.g., *Embracing Stakeholder Centricity in M&A*
- **"Avoiding [bad outcome]: The Power of [Y]"** — e.g., *Avoiding the Courtroom: The Power of Pre-Litigation Dispute Resolution*
- **"Why [established belief] is Wrong About [topic]"** — contrarian variant
- **"The [N] [things] Every [founder/buyer/seller] Should [know/avoid] Before [milestone]"** — list-promise variant

These are advisory headlines — not curiosity-gap clickbait. The reader can predict the angle before reading.

## 6. Vocabulary

### Words & phrases to use
- "In my experience advising founders…"
- "The hidden cost of…"
- "Counterintuitively…"
- "Before you sign…"
- "Three things most founders miss about…"
- "Anonymized: a client I advised last quarter…"
- "Stakeholder," "principal," "counterparty"
- "Diligence," "earnout," "rep and warranty," "indemnification cap"

### Words to avoid
- "Litigation" used casually — Doug helps clients *avoid* it (per "Avoiding the Courtroom")
- "Game-changer," "level up," "10x" — wrong register
- "Disrupt"
- "Pursuant to," "heretofore," "the undersigned" — formal legalese in posts
- "Hustle," "grind," "no days off"
- Anything political

## 7. Post Format Conventions (LinkedIn-native)

```
[Hook — 1-2 lines]
[blank line]
[Body — 3-7 short paragraphs, blank line between each]
[blank line]
[CTA — usually a question or "DM me to discuss"]
[blank line]
[3-5 hashtags max]
```

- Hook should land an idea, not tease one
- Generous whitespace; LinkedIn truncates around 200 characters → first 2 lines do the heavy lift
- Link in comments, not in post body (LinkedIn deprioritizes link posts)
- **No emoji bullets** (that's the LinkedIn anti-pattern Doug rises above)

## 8. Visual Identity

Every post graphic in the reference set is a **Scale LLP-branded title card**:

- **Background:** dark teal-charcoal (`#1F5560`) or a stock corporate image (skyscrapers, glass towers, sunsets over cities) with a teal overlay
- **Wordmark:** "SCALE LLP" top-left or bottom-right corner, cream-mint (`#D8EBE5`)
- **Title:** Large display sans-serif, cream-mint, often broken across 2-3 lines for rhythm
- **Subtitle:** Smaller, lighter, often italic or with an accent stroke
- **Attribution chip:** "Doug Mitchell, Partner" in a small box or footer band
- **No accent color** beyond the cream-mint on teal (intentional — quiet)
- **No emoji, no flourishes, no gradient text**

Aspect ratio: square (1200×1200) for LinkedIn feed. Landscape (1200×627) for article-style.
