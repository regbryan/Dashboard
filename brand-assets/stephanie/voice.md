## 1. Brand Overview

Stephanie Perez is a personal-brand mortgage loan consultant under Answer Home Loans (AHL), based in El Dorado Hills, CA (Sacramento Valley). Her positioning is deeply personal: she's a Sacramento Valley native, Elk Grove grad, Cal State Sacramento alum, and spent **15 years in law enforcement** before becoming a loan officer. That backstory — integrity-trained, people-first, protective — is the emotional core of her brand.

On Instagram, Stephanie sounds like a trusted friend who also happens to be your loan officer. Every post is first-person singular ("I", "me", "my"). The feed alternates between personal-brand storytelling carousels ("The Truth About Working With Me"), client celebration posts ("IN CONTRACT"), and seasonal/inspirational touches. The voice is warm, honest, unhurried, and values-forward: *"I won't promise things just to win your business. If something isn't realistic, I'll tell you — early and honestly."*

Visually, the feed is **soft, feminine, and intentionally calming** — dominated by muted dusty blue overlay cards with white serif text on warm lifestyle photography and Stephanie's own professional photos. Think "trusted guide with great taste" — not corporate lender, not fintech startup.

**Critical CSS correction:** The v0 starter listed earthy tan `#B69D81` as primary from CSS. Across 14 actual reference posts, tan appears only in Stephanie's wardrobe and interior settings — the **actual design palette is dusty steel blue + white + charcoal**. The tan is ambient, not structural.

*Confidence: **High** on visuals (14 reference posts with consistent patterns). **Medium** on voice (inferred from on-post text — no captions pasted yet).*

## 4. Voice Pillars

1. **First-person authentic.** This is a PERSONAL brand. Every post is "I," "me," "my." Not "we" or "the team." Stephanie IS the brand. *"I've been through the mortgage process myself — I know how overwhelming it can feel."*
2. **Values-forward, not product-forward.** Posts lead with integrity, honesty, and trust — not rates, programs, or products. The product is Stephanie herself. *"I won't promise things just to win your business. If something isn't realistic, I'll tell you — early and honestly."*
3. **Calm and unhurried.** No urgency, no FOMO, no "rates are dropping ACT NOW!" She creates a safe, patient space. The word "calm" appears explicitly in her posts: *"calm guidance."*
4. **Relational closer.** Posts often end with a warm, personal invitation — not a hard CTA. *"I'd love to help you navigate your next move. ♥"*

*Confidence: **Medium-High** — voice pillars are well-evidenced from the carousel series "The Truth About Working With Me" (7 slides of first-person copy). Upgrade to High once full captions are pasted.*

## 7. Vocabulary

**Use (on-brand):**
- "I'd love to help you navigate…"
- "calm guidance"
- "honest," "honestly," "honesty"
- "I've been through this myself"
- "confident decision"
- "advocate for you"
- "educate you," "set clear expectations"
- "communicate openly"
- "your next move"
- "in contract!" (celebration)

**Avoid (off-brand):**
- "Apply now!" / "Limited time!" / "Act fast!" (pressure)
- "We" or "our team" (it's personal — always "I")
- "Crushing it" / "killing the game" (bro energy)
- "Best rates guaranteed" (compliance risk + off-tone)
- Corporate speak: "leverage," "solutions," "synergy"
- Emoji spam (keep to 0–2, preferring ♥ and 🏡)
- Fear-based messaging about rates or market
- Bare jargon: LTV, DTI, PMI without plain-English gloss

**Define on first use:** *equity, pre-approval, refinance, escrow, closing costs, NMLS, DRE.*

## 8. Visual Identity

### Color palette (confirmed 2026-04-11)

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary accent** | Deep Blue | `#3D5A80` | Signature brand color — text overlay banners, cards, hero headline backgrounds, services checklist. The editorial/serious anchor of the palette. |
| **Secondary accent** | Light Sky Blue | `#98C1D9` | Softer blue for secondary cards, dividers, background fills, lighter overlays. Creates depth when paired with deep blue. |
| **Ice accent** | Ice Blue | `#E0FBFC` | Very light blue wash — subtle background tints, card fills, hover states, breathing-room panels. Almost white with a cool kiss. |
| **Background** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background, card fills, polaroid frames, text on light backgrounds |
| **Text** | Black | `#000000` | All body text, headlines on light backgrounds, credentials, contact info |

**Important:** The palette is a blue tonal family — deep to ice — on white and black. It pairs with warm lifestyle photography of Stephanie to create a "calm, trustworthy, feminine" feel. The warm tan in Stephanie's wardrobe and interior settings is ambient (not a design color) and provides the warmth that keeps the blues from feeling clinical.

### Typography tiers

| Tier | Style | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Display serif** | Elegant serif, mixed case or ALL CAPS (similar to a Bodoni/Didot/Playfair weight) | Hero headlines on blue overlays: "THE TRUTH ABOUT WORKING WITH ME", "THE QUIET POWER OF EQUITY". White text on blue. |
| **Body serif** | Lighter-weight serif, centered, sentence case | Carousel slide body text, quote text, "I've been through the mortgage process myself…" |
| **Script/handwritten** | Flowing personal script (similar to the AHL logo script) | "IN CONTRACT" headers, "Stephanie Perez" signature, "my services", event titles. The personal/celebratory typeface. |
| **Clean sans** | Regular weight sans for credentials and details | "LOAN CONSULTANT", "NMLS 1081264", "DRE 02423262", contact info, checklist items |
| **AHL logo** | Distinctive script "ahl" + spaced sans "ANSWER HOME LOANS" | Co-brand element on IN CONTRACT posts, checklist, events |

*The serif-on-blue-overlay is the core Stephanie visual signature. The script is for celebrations and the personal touch. Sans is purely functional (credentials, contact info).*

### Logo / personal brand mark

- **Primary:** No standalone "SP" logo exists. The brand IS Stephanie's face + name in script + AHL co-brand.
- **Headshot:** Circular crop of Stephanie (blonde, warm smile) — used on every IN CONTRACT post and the services checklist.
- **Script signature:** "Stephanie Perez" in flowing script — personal mark, appears below the circular headshot.
- **Credentials block:** "LOAN CONSULTANT / NMLS 1081264 / DRE 02423262" in small clean sans.
- **AHL co-brand:** Script "ahl" + "ANSWER HOME LOANS" + "STEVENSON LENDING GROUP" logo. Appears on IN CONTRACT posts, services checklists, and event invitations. Does NOT appear on personal-brand carousels or inspirational posts.

### Photography direction

- **Stephanie's own professional photos** are the primary visual asset — she appears in at least 5 of 14 posts. Blue denim dress or tan blazer, on porches, in homes, at her laptop. Warm natural light, relaxed posing, genuine smile.
- **Lifestyle stock** for carousel slides — sunflowers in vase, planners, bedrooms with laptop and coffee, cream mugs on magazines, pillows, vintage phones. Warm, bright, feminine, "lifestyle blogger" aesthetic.
- **Property photos** for IN CONTRACT posts — real listing photos inside the polaroid frame template.
- **Event photos** — venue collages for appreciation events.
- **Avoid:** corporate stock, dark/moody, harsh lighting, generic office settings, stock photos of "diverse team in conference room."

### Layout conventions

- **Instagram feed:** 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait)
- **Blue overlay cards** are the Stephanie fingerprint — semi-transparent or solid deep blue `#3D5A80` or light sky blue `#98C1D9` rectangles placed over photos with white serif text inside
- **IN CONTRACT template** is locked: script "IN CONTRACT" top → polaroid white frame → property photo → circular headshot bottom-left → script "Stephanie Perez" + credentials → AHL logo bottom-right → warm blurred background
- **Carousel slides** use consistent dusty blue banner position with white serif — the banner moves around but the color/font treatment is consistent
- **Lots of breathing room** — never crammed, never cluttered
- **Safe zone:** keep key text 8% from every edge

*Confidence: **High** on palette, typography, and layout patterns (14 reference posts with very consistent treatment). **Medium** on exact font names — confirm with Stephanie or her designer.*
