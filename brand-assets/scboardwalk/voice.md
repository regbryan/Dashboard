# Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk Crew — Brand Voice Guidelines

> **Status:** v1, evidence-based. Built from kit.md + 9 reference-posts on @beachboardwalkjobs. Visual + voice both well-established from existing graphics; voice is recruitment-focused and bold-callout-heavy.
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-25
> **Platform:** Instagram — recruitment sub-account for the Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk

---

## 1. Brand Overview

@beachboardwalkjobs is the **recruitment voice** of the iconic Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk — the 100+ year-old California amusement park. This is a sub-account purpose-built for hiring: not a general boardwalk-marketing account, not a customer-experience account.

The job is to convert local students (16+) and seasonal-worker candidates into applicants — and convert hires into proud, posting alumni who tell their friends.

The brand voice is **summer-camp-counselor confident**: warm, energetic, fun-but-real about the work. Pride of place ("the iconic Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk") is foundational — applicants want to say *that's* where they work this summer.

## 2. Audience

- **Primary:** Local Santa Cruz / Bay Area high-school + college students 16+. First-job, summer-job, or stepping-stone-to-something-bigger applicants.
- **Secondary:** **International college students** evaluating the Work & Travel Program (30+ year legacy — Brazilian, Eastern European, and Asian students annually).
- **Tertiary:** Adults seeking flexible seasonal hospitality work.
- **Quaternary:** Parents of teen applicants (who actually read the job descriptions).

## 3. Voice Pillars

### 1. **Bold callout energy**
Every post earns attention in 0.3 seconds. Big stacked words ("WE ARE HIRING", "HIRING HIRING HIRING"), megaphone graphics, exclamation-mark CTAs. The feed should feel like a hype reel, not a careers page.

### 2. **Peer-to-peer warmth**
Tone is "come work with us this summer" — never "submit your application." The reference posts use first names + roles for employees ("EDGAR RAMIREZ · INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY HARDWARE TECHNICIAN") because the Boardwalk Crew is a *who*, not a *what*.

### 3. **Perks over pay**
Reference posts emphasize **Referral Bonus**, **Season Passes** (the Work & Play perk), unlimited day-wristbands for friends and family, and the experience of working at an iconic local landmark. Pay is rarely the headline.

### 4. **Pride of place**
"The iconic Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk" is the implicit subtitle of every post. Palm-tree sunsets, the Looff Carousel, the Giant Dipper coaster — these aren't backdrops, they're the *reason* people want to work here.

### 5. **Specific roles, real urgency**
"Director of Boardwalk Operations" / "Now Hiring Ride Operators" — name the role, name the timing. Hiring posts perform when the reader can answer "is there something for me here?" in 2 seconds.

## 4. We Are / We Are Not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Sunny, energetic, callout-loud | Corporate careers-page polite |
| Peer-to-peer ("come work with us") | Top-down ("submit your application") |
| Perks- and culture-forward | Pay-forward |
| Employee-face-forward | Faceless / generic stock |
| Bold poster-style graphics | Quiet minimalist whitespace |
| Iconic-local-landmark proud | Anywhere-amusement-park generic |

## 5. Tone-by-Context Matrix

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Hiring callout post | Loud, urgent, specific role. Megaphone energy. |
| Employee spotlight | Warm, name-and-role-forward, mini-bio with one fun fact |
| Perks reveal | Excited, generous, "you get all this" |
| Referral bonus | Direct, dollar-amount-clear |
| Hiring fair / event | Time-and-place-explicit, "walk in get hired" energy |
| Work & Travel Program | International-welcoming, 30-year-legacy proud |
| Reflective / off-season | Quieter, "we'll see you in spring," anticipation |

## 6. Vocabulary

### Words & phrases to use
- "Come work with us"
- "Crew" (not "staff," not "employees" — *the Boardwalk Crew*)
- "Iconic"
- "Work where others vacation"
- "Free park access," "Season Passes," "unlimited wristbands"
- "Summer push," "hiring season"
- "Walk-in interviews," "instant hire"
- "Work & Play," "Work & Travel"
- Real names: "Edgar from IT," "Maya from Rides"
- "16 and up," "no experience needed"

### Words to avoid
- "Apply now" (overused, corporate) — prefer "Come work with us" or "DM us your name"
- "Submit your application" — too formal for IG audience
- "Career opportunity" — too LinkedIn
- "We are seeking…" — passive corporate voice
- "Competitive pay" — perks come first
- "Family" as a description of the crew (overused, cringe)
- Em dashes as connective tissue in captions

## 7. Visual Identity

Confirmed from reference-posts:

### Graphics
- **Bold poster-style** — large stacked headline text, contrasting blocks (cerulean blue + sunshine yellow + orange), drop shadows on words for depth
- **Megaphone illustrations** with motion lines (vector style)
- **Hand-drawn-feel mixed with photo backdrops** — palm-tree sunsets, beach umbrellas, the iconic boardwalk skyline
- **Brand mark** ("Beach Boardwalk" oval / shield) bottom-center on most posts
- **CTA buttons** ("APPLY NOW") in orange-on-blue, rounded-pill

### Employee spotlights
- Half-body photo of the employee (often holding a relevant prop — a championship belt for IT Edgar, a tray for foodservice crew)
- Big "GET TO KNOW" or "MEET THE CREW" header
- Name in display caps + role label below
- Cerulean blue background, yellow accent stripes

### Locked palette (per `brand.json`)
- Primary (cerulean): `#1070B0`
- Secondary (Giant Dipper orange): `#F07010`
- Accent (boardwalk lights yellow): `#F0D010`
- Background: `#FFFFFF`

### Photography
- Real crew in real uniform, mid-shift or on a break, smiling/laughing
- Sunset beach + boardwalk backdrops when possible (palm-tree silhouettes are gold)
- Movement, energy, sun, smiling — never staged/posed-against-a-wall studio shots

## 12. Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet

- **Platform:** Instagram, 3x/week Tu/Th/Sat
- **Format:** Image (70%), Reel (30%) — bold poster graphics + crew spotlights
- **Voice:** Bold callout energy, peer-to-peer warmth, perks-forward, pride-of-place
- **Visual:** Big stacked words, cerulean + orange + yellow, megaphones, palm-tree sunsets, real crew faces
- **Hashtags:** 5-8 per post, identity + role + location
- **Tone test:** If it sounds like a college peer hyping you to apply to the most-fun-job-this-summer, it's right. If it sounds like a corporate careers page or generic "join our family" pitch, rewrite.
