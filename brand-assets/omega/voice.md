# Omega Mortgage Group — Brand Voice & Visual Identity Guidelines

> **Source:** omglending.com + 11 @omglending reference posts (screenshots, 2026-04-09)
> **Scan date:** 2026-04-09
> **Scope:** Voice, visual identity, layout archetypes, and publishing cheat sheet for @omglending Instagram feed.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

Omega Mortgage Group is a white-glove concierge mortgage lender headquartered in Reno, NV with 7 branches across California and Nevada. Their positioning line, verbatim from the homepage, is **"Finally, A Lender You'd Refer Your Friends To…"** — which captures the whole brand in one sentence: they want to be the trusted family friend, not the transactional rate shop.

On Instagram they sound like a warm, senior loan officer walking a first-time homebuyer through the process with patience. Every post answers *"what's in it for you as a buyer?"* with a concrete, educational payoff — reasons, steps, requirements, savings tactics. The tone is professional but never stiff, reassuring but never pushy, and the photography skews warm, aspirational, and explicitly diverse (interracial couples, families at the dinner table, elderly couples, multi-generation homes).

Visually, the feed is **editorial and premium**, not corporate: large serif display headlines, script/italic accents for connecting words, navy `#005181` as the dominant color, and real photography doing most of the heavy lifting. Think "wealth management newsletter meets hometown realtor" — not "fintech startup."

*Confidence: **Medium-High** on visuals (11 reference posts + site), Medium on voice (captions not yet pasted — inferred from post body copy only).*

## 4. Voice Pillars

1. **Warm senior guide.** Speaks like an experienced loan officer who has walked hundreds of families through this. Calm, patient, never rushed. *Example on-brand phrase from posts: "The best mortgage type depends on your financial situation, goals, and preferences."*
2. **Educational, not salesy.** Every post gives something away for free — a number, a reason, a tip, a question to ask yourself. No hard-sell, no "CALL NOW!", no urgency tactics.
3. **Plain-English translator.** Terms like *LTV, DTI, escrow, amortization* get translated on first use. If it needs a glossary, it needs a sentence of plain English first.
4. **Aspirational but grounded.** Photography shows real families in real homes, not luxury fantasies. The dream is attainable — "Your dream home is possible" — not "mansion of your imagination."

*Confidence: **Medium** — voice pillars inferred from on-post body copy in 11 references + homepage tone. Upgrade to High once 5–10 real captions are pasted to `reference-posts/captions.md`.*

## 5. We Are / We Are Not

| We ARE | We are NOT |
|---|---|
| Warm, patient, educational | Hype, urgency, FOMO |
| First-person plural or second-person ("we'll walk you through…", "you") | Third-person distant corporate |
| Editorial serif display + clean sans body | Loud bold-sans-shouting-in-all-caps |
| Photo-first storytelling with real families | Stock-photo cheese or emoji-spam |
| Plain-English translator of loan jargon | Jargon-heavy wall of acronyms |
| Numbered lists and clear steps | Long paragraphs with no structure |
| Diverse, inclusive photography | Homogeneous stock imagery |
| Navy `#005181` dominant, cream breathing room | Bright primary colors or rainbow gradients |
| Compliance-footer-always, NMLS always visible | Posts without licensing disclosure |
| Partnering language ("let's figure this out together") | Transactional language ("apply today for instant approval") |

## 7. Vocabulary

**Use (on-brand):**
- "your dream home"
- "let's walk through it"
- "here's what that means for your monthly payment"
- "finally, a lender…"
- "thoughtful guidance"
- "personal and responsive"
- "smart move"
- "your partner in the process"
- "white-glove service"
- "rapid and reliable"

**Avoid (off-brand):**
- "apply now!" / "limited time!" / "act fast!"
- "instant approval" (overpromise, compliance risk)
- "cheapest rates" (price-only framing)
- "no brainer" / "slam dunk" (bro-speak)
- "game-changer" / "disrupt" (fintech-bro energy)
- "easy peasy" (too casual for compliance-regulated industry)
- Emoji clutter (keep to 0–2 per caption, never in headlines)
- Bare acronyms: *LTV, DTI, PMI, ARM, HELOC* without a plain-English gloss on first use

**Define on first use:** *LTV = loan-to-value, DTI = debt-to-income, PMI = private mortgage insurance, escrow, amortization, points, APR, ARM, HELOC*.

## 8. Visual Identity

### Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Omega Navy | `#005181` | Dominant — header pills, CTA bars, full backgrounds, numbered-circle rings, logo Ω monogram, "OMEGA" wordmark |
| **Near-Black** | Omega Black | `#231F20` | Logo wordmark ("MORTGAGE GROUP"), dark text on light backgrounds *(confirmed by client 2026-04-11)* |
| **Near-White BG** | Near-White | `#FEFEFE` | Default light background, card fills, listicle backgrounds, breathing room *(confirmed by client 2026-04-11 — replaces earlier #F8FAFA estimate)* |
| **Accent (reserved)** | Omega Gold | `#FDD314` | Reserved for 5-star review graphics and sparing highlights ONLY — does NOT dominate feed posts |
| **Text on dark** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines and body over navy |
| **Text on light** | Navy | `#005181` | Headlines and body over cream/photos |

**Important:** across 11 reference posts, gold was **not visible in a single feed post**. Gold appears to be reserved for specific review-quote cards. Do not use gold as a primary field unless replicating a review post. Navy + near-white + photo is the default.

### Typography tiers

| Tier | Style | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Display headline** | Elegant high-contrast serif (Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond / Didot style), mixed case | Hero numerals ("6", "3"), topic headlines ("USDA LOANS", "REVERSE MORTGAGES") |
| **Script accent** | Flowing script or italic script (Allura / Playlist / Pinyon style) | Connecting words ("Why", "On Your Mortgage") — paired with the display serif |
| **Subhead / eyebrow** | Light/regular sans-serif ALL CAPS with letter-spacing (tracking ~100) | Category labels, "WAYS TO SAVE", "WHY REFINANCE?" |
| **Body** | Clean sans (Inter / Helvetica Neue / Montserrat regular) | 1-sentence explainers in numbered lists and photo-card bodies |
| **Logo** | Circular Ω monogram + "OMEGA / MORTGAGE / GROUP" stacked geometric sans | Top-left, top-center, or navy-bar header |
| **Compliance footer band** | Tiny regular sans, ALL CAPS, white on navy `#005181`, **burned into the image itself** (not just caption) | Full-width band flush to bottom of every post + every carousel slide. Full disclosure text per §14. Updated 2026-05-05 — image is now the source of truth, not the caption. |

*The serif-plus-script pairing is the single most distinctive visual signature on the feed. It is what separates Omega from every other generic mortgage brand. Preserve it.*

### Logo usage

- Primary: circular Ω monogram + **OMEGA / MORTGAGE / GROUP** stacked wordmark
- Always on every post (top-left, top-center, or inside a navy header bar)
- Reversed white version for use on navy or photo backgrounds
- Do not stretch, recolor, or put the logo on a busy photo without a solid-color pill behind it

### Photography direction

- **Real families, real light.** No glossy corporate stock.
- **Diverse and inclusive.** The reference set includes interracial couples, elderly couples, young families with babies, multi-generation kitchens.
- **Warm natural light.** Golden hour, window light, cream interiors.
- **Subjects in homes, not offices.** Kitchens, living rooms, front porches, dining tables — not conference rooms.
- **Moments, not poses.** Handing over keys, looking at a laptop together, sitting around a dinner table.
- **Landscape context shots** are OK for programs like USDA (barns, fields, mountain scenes).

### Layout conventions

- **Instagram feed:** 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait)
- **Compliance footer band** at the bottom of every post (thin strip, tiny type, state licensing numbers)
- **Logo lockup** always visible — top-left, top-center, or in a navy header bar
- **Numbered lists** use thin-ring navy circles with navy numerals inside (NOT filled navy circles with white numerals — that's an IEC/CSC pattern; Omega's circles are hollow and elegant)
- **Safe zone:** keep key text 8% from every edge

*Confidence: **High** on the palette, typography pairing, and layout patterns (verified across 11 reference posts). **Medium** on exact named fonts — brand team should confirm the specific serif + script typefaces in use.*

## 14. Required Compliance Disclaimer

**Updated 2026-05-05 — placement changed.** The disclaimer must now be **burned into the image itself** as a footer band on every post (feed, carousel slides, reels cover frames). It is no longer acceptable to put this in the caption only. Captions may still include it, but the **image is the source of truth** for compliance.

**Exact disclaimer text (must match verbatim):**

> Infinity Equity Group, LLC f/k/a Infinity Equity Group, Inc. dba Omega Mortgage Group. | Licensed by the Department of Financial Protection and Innovation under the California Residential Mortgage Lending Act #441DBO-106851 and NMLS #1253874 AZ license #1007664 | CA 41DBO-106851 | CO none | FL MLD2263 | ID MBL9351 | NV 4482 | OR ML-5506 | TN 1253874 | TX none | WA CL-1253784 | www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org

### On-image footer band — design spec

Reference: see the FHA Loan post (sample provided 2026-05-05) — replicate this band on every output.

- **Position:** flush to the bottom edge of the canvas, full width, no margin
- **Background:** Omega navy `#005181` (solid)
- **Text color:** white `#FFFFFF`
- **Font:** sans-serif (Montserrat or Source Sans Pro), regular weight, ALL CAPS
- **Font size:** small enough to fit the entire disclosure on **2 lines max** at 1080px wide (typically 9–11pt equivalent at 1:1, slightly larger at 4:5)
- **Letter-spacing:** tight, ~0 to +10
- **Line layout:** break naturally between license entries; pipes (`|`) separate states
- **Padding:** ~10–14px top/bottom inside the band
- **Always present:** every static image, every carousel slide (yes, every slide — not just slide 1), every reel cover. Mid-reel frames are exempt unless the reel is a single static still.
- **Never:** crop it, fade it, reduce opacity, or place content over it. The band is sacred space.

### Caption usage (now optional but still permitted)

Because the disclaimer is on the image, the caption no longer has to carry it. Captions can drop the long disclosure block. NMLS #1253874 should still be discoverable somewhere on the profile (bio or pinned post).
