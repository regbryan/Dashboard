## 1. Brand Overview

Blitz Organization is a professional home organizing service based in El Dorado Hills, CA (Sacramento area). Their tagline trio — **"DESIGN | ORGANIZE | RELOCATE"** — captures the full scope: they don't just declutter, they design systems, organize spaces, and help people through moves. The homepage says it best: *"Life happens. Things can get messy and start to pile up."* followed by their action CTA: *"Let's get this party started!"*

On Instagram, Blitz sounds like your calm, encouraging friend who's really good at organizing — the one who comes over, rolls up her sleeves, and makes your pantry look like a magazine spread without making you feel bad about the mess. The voice is warm, question-driven, empathetic, and sprinkled with just enough personality to feel human. Posts are overwhelmingly photo-first: real client spaces (garages, pantries, closets) interspersed with dreamy lifestyle stock for mood-setting question hooks.

Visually, the feed is **soft, feminine, and intentionally calming** — peachy-pink accent on white, script/handwritten typography for hooks, and abundant natural light. The aesthetic is "organized life" in every sense: clean lines, clear bins, labeled jars, soft pastels, and breathing room. Think "curated Pinterest board meets your best friend's Instagram story."

*Confidence: **Medium-High** on visuals (9 reference posts + site). **Medium** on voice (inferred from on-post text and website copy — no captions pasted yet).*

## 4. Voice Pillars

1. **Warm encourager.** Speaks like a supportive friend, never a lecturer. Meets people where they are — no shame, no guilt about the mess. *"Dear busy mom…"* is peak Blitz voice — empathetic, personal, inviting.
2. **Question-driven.** Posts frequently open with a question that the audience is already asking themselves: *"How does clutter affect your mental health?"* *"What is the best way to maintain an organized space long term?"* *"How can a professional organizer help a real estate agent?"* This creates engagement and positions Blitz as the answer.
3. **Practical tipster.** Every educational post gives a specific, actionable tip — not vague advice. *"Clear bins give you visibility. When you can see exactly what you have, there's no digging, no duplicates."* Concrete, useful, implementable today.
4. **Calm confidence.** Never brags, never shames. Just quietly demonstrates expertise through real results (organizing photos) and accessible explanations. *"We don't just organize stuff, we organize decisions."*

*Confidence: **Medium** — voice pillars inferred from on-post body copy in 9 references + website. Upgrade to High once 5–10 real captions are pasted to `reference-posts/captions.md`.*

## 7. Vocabulary

**Use (on-brand):**
- "maximize your space"
- "let's get this party started"
- "life happens"
- "we're here to help"
- "calm," "lighter," "energized"
- "functional and easy to maintain"
- "a home for everything"
- "visibility" (for clear bins)
- "reset your home"
- "dear busy mom"

**Avoid (off-brand):**
- "messy," "dirty," "disaster" (shaming language)
- "hoarder" (clinical, judgmental)
- "KonMari" without context (avoid trademarked method references)
- "BOOK NOW!" / "LIMITED SPOTS!" (pressure tactics)
- "life-changing" / "game-changer" (overblown)
- Corporate speak: "leverage," "optimize" (too formal — use "use every inch" instead)
- Emoji spam (keep to 0–2 per caption, pastels like 🤍 or 🌿 preferred)

**Define on first use:** *declutter vs. organize (different things), system (an organizing system, not tech), zone (a functional area within a room).*

## 8. Visual Identity

### Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dominant accent** | Blitz Dusty Rose | `#ECB7B9` | Signature brand color — text banners/pills, CTA buttons, highlight bars, logo circle accent. The single most distinctive Blitz color. |
| **Secondary accent** | Sage Green | `#9CAF9C` | Organic, calming complement — secondary banners, icon backgrounds, dividers, subtle accents that pair with the dusty rose |
| **Background / Neutral** | Warm Beige | `#DAD6CF` | Soft warm neutral — alternate background panels, card fills, photo overlays. Warmer than pure white for a lived-in feel. |
| **Background** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background for text-card areas, breathing room, and clean layouts |
| **Text** | Charcoal Gray | `#595959` | All body text, clean sans headlines, secondary text, and UI elements |

**Important:** The palette is **dusty rose + sage green + warm beige + white + charcoal**. No navy, no red, no bright or neon colors. The dusty rose `#ECB7B9` replaces the former peachy-pink `#FEAEA0` as the dominant brand accent. The sage green `#9CAF9C` adds an organic, nature-inspired complement. The warm beige `#DAD6CF` provides a softer alternative to pure white backgrounds.

### Typography tiers

| Tier | Style | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Script/handwritten headline** | Casual flowing script (not formal calligraphy — more like a friendly handwritten note). Think Playlist Script / Sacramento / Caveat style. | Hook words, question starters, emotional phrases ("maximize", "How can a", "Dear busy mom", "clean-up", "What"). The single most distinctive Blitz typeface. |
| **Clean sans headline** | Regular-weight sans (not bold/heavy — think Montserrat Light/Regular, Open Sans, or Lato) | Secondary headlines paired with script, body text on pink banners, CTA buttons |
| **Bold sans** | Bold weight of the same clean sans | Used sparingly — only for emphasis words ("Did you know?") or promo posts ("YOUR DREAM SPACE, MADE POSSIBLE") |
| **Logo** | Stylized "B" monogram + "BLITZ ORGANIZATION" in spaced sans + "DESIGN | ORGANIZE | RELOCATE" tagline | Top-left, top-center, or bottom-right on posts |

*The script-plus-light-sans pairing is the core Blitz typographic identity. Script carries the emotion, sans carries the information. Heavy bold-sans-all-caps is reserved for rare promo posts only.*

### Logo usage

- Primary: stylized B monogram + **BLITZ ORGANIZATION** + **DESIGN | ORGANIZE | RELOCATE** tagline
- Appears top-left, top-center, or bottom-right depending on layout
- Dark version on white/cream backgrounds, white version on dark photo backgrounds
- Keep small and unobtrusive — Blitz posts let photos and text breathe, the logo doesn't dominate

### Photography direction

- **Real organizing photos** are the primary visual asset — actual pantries, garages, closets, shelves after a Blitz session. Clear bins, labeled jars, matching baskets, everything in its place.
- **Lifestyle stock** for mood/question-hook posts — flowers, journals on wooden stools, clothing racks in soft pastel tones, airy bright interiors with french doors and natural light.
- **Warm, bright, natural light.** Soft shadows, clean surfaces, breathing room.
- **Pastel/neutral palette** in actual photo content — no harsh primary colors.
- **Avoid:** dark/moody, cluttered "before" shots without context, harsh fluorescent garage lighting, corporate office stock.

### Layout conventions

- **Instagram feed:** 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait)
- **Dusty rose `#ECB7B9` text banners/pills** overlay photos or sit beside them — these are the Blitz visual fingerprint. Sage green `#9CAF9C` and warm beige `#DAD6CF` for secondary panels.
- **Lots of white space** — layouts breathe. Text is never crammed.
- **Script text is always left-aligned or centered, never justified**
- **Safe zone:** keep key text 8% from every edge

*Confidence: **High** on the palette, script typography, and photo style (verified across 9 reference posts). **Medium** on exact font names — brand team should confirm the specific script typeface.*

## 13. Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet

**Before you publish, confirm:**

- [ ] Dusty rose `#ECB7B9` accent is present (banner, pill, button, or highlight)
- [ ] Background is white or very light (not dark, not navy, not busy)
- [ ] Script/handwritten font used for hook or emotional phrase
- [ ] Clean sans used for body/explainer text (not bold-sans-shouting)
- [ ] Photo is either a real organizing space OR a soft lifestyle/stock mood photo
- [ ] Photo has warm, bright, natural light
- [ ] Layout breathes — no text cramming, generous white space
- [ ] Tone is warm and encouraging, never shaming or judgmental about mess
- [ ] Caption opens with a question or empathetic statement (not a command)
- [ ] CTA is soft ("follow for more", "DM us", "save this") — never pressure
- [ ] 0–2 emoji, preferring pastels (🤍 🌿 ✨) over loud ones
- [ ] 8–12 hashtags, mixing always-on + local + service + lifestyle
- [ ] Logo visible but unobtrusive (top-left, top-center, or bottom-right)
